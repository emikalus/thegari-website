# GARI Institutional Mission Statement

**Status:** Proposed for adoption; not yet formally approved by the Board of Directors.

**Date drafted:** October 18, 2026

## Mission statement

The mission of the Global Accessibility Research Institute (GARI) is to advance independence, inclusion, and quality of life for people with disabilities through research, innovation, and the development and evaluation of accessible technologies. GARI works to identify barriers, develop practical solutions, and generate evidence that informs technology, public policy, and inclusive practices.
