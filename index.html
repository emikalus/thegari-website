Our Mission

Technology should expand possibility—not create new barriers.

GARI is a nonprofit research organization working at the intersection of accessibility, systems engineering, artificial intelligence, and lived experience.

We investigate meaningful problems, build practical solutions, and evaluate whether those solutions work for the people they are intended to serve.

Accessible innovation is not only about what technology can do. It is about whether people can use it independently, safely, and reliably.
Research & Innovation

Work rooted in real-world needs.

Our research agenda explores accessible transportation, independent caregiving, inclusive learning, and the impact of AI on people with disabilities.

Future research direction

Accessible Caregiving & Learning

How can accessible, voice-based tools help blind caregivers independently interpret information, support children's learning, and manage complex caregiving tasks? Future studies may examine concepts such as Insulin Buddy for diabetes education and EchoReader for reading support. These are research concepts, not currently offered services or validated interventions.

Research & development

Transit Accessibility Network

An accessible passenger-to-vehicle interaction concept exploring how blind riders could identify the correct transit vehicle, communicate boarding needs, receive acknowledgments, and stay aware of their trip.

Planned research publication

AI Accessibility Report

A planned institutional research initiative examining how artificial intelligence affects the lives, opportunities, and independence of people with disabilities.

Our Approach

From a need to measurable impact.

We combine disciplined engineering with user-centered accessibility research. Each phase informs what comes next.

01 · Research

Understand barriers, users, operating environments, and risks.

02 · Develop

Translate needs into requirements, designs, and prototypes.

03 · Pilot

Evaluate concepts under increasingly realistic conditions.

04 · Learn

Measure usability, reliability, safety, and opportunities to improve.
